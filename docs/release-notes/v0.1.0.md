# Tumon 0.1.0 (first preview)

First preview of Tumon: a camera called **Tumon Camera** that TeamTalk lists and sets up by itself, fed by your laptop
camera or webcam. Published 2026-11-28.

### Added
- **Tumon Camera.** A permanent Windows 11 camera that offers YUY2, so TeamTalk fills in its resolution list without
  typing a custom video format.
- **The Tumon app.** Choose your camera and press **Start**. Your real camera turns on only while some program is using
  Tumon Camera. Tray icon, English and Bahasa Indonesia.
- **Installer** that registers the camera and removes it again on uninstall. Without a running source the camera shows
  colour bars.

### Known limitations
- Tested on one Windows 11 PC with its built-in camera and TeamTalk 5.23; USB webcams are not yet tested.
- **Not code-signed**, so Windows SmartScreen may warn.
- The TeamTalk **phone** app was seen showing red and blue swapped (a computer receiver showed correct colours), and
  some lag on the phone.
- TeamTalk remembers a camera by its position in the camera list. If cameras are added or removed, select Tumon Camera
  again.
- Not yet tried by a screen-reader user, although the controls report their names and roles correctly.
- Another program running as a signed-in user could send its own pictures through Tumon Camera (see
  [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md)).
- No effects and no phone-as-camera yet.

### Install
Needs Windows 11 (build 22000 or newer), 64-bit, and administrator rights. Download **`Tumon-Setup-0.1.0.exe`** and run
it; if SmartScreen warns, check the SHA-256 on the GitHub release page, then choose *More info* and *Run anyway*. In
TeamTalk, *Preferences › Video Capture*: device **Tumon Camera**, **640x480, FPS 30**, image format **YUY2**, then turn
on video transmission. To remove everything: *Settings › Apps › Installed apps › Tumon › Uninstall*.

## Bahasa Indonesia

Tumon membuat TeamTalk bisa memakai kamera laptop atau webcam, termasuk kamera yang tidak bisa diatur sendiri oleh
TeamTalk. Perlu Windows 11. Jalankan `Tumon-Setup-0.1.0.exe` (belum ditandatangani, jadi SmartScreen mungkin
memperingatkan: cocokkan SHA-256, lalu *More info › Run anyway*). Buka Tumon, pilih kamera, tekan **Mulai**. Di
TeamTalk pilih **Tumon Camera**, 640x480 FPS 30, format YUY2, lalu aktifkan transmisi video.

Keterbatasan: baru diuji di satu PC dan webcam USB belum diuji; di aplikasi TeamTalk HP merah dan biru pernah tertukar
dan ada jeda; kalau daftar kamera berubah, pilih ulang Tumon Camera di TeamTalk.
