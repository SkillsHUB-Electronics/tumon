# Privacy

*Design statement for a project that has no release yet. It will be updated before any version that
sends data anywhere.*

- **Video is processed on your PC.** Frames from your cameras are passed from the Tumon app to the virtual
  camera on the same computer and then to the apps you choose. Tumon does not record video to disk and
  does not upload it.
- **Phone as a camera (planned)** sends video from your phone to your PC over your local network or USB.
  It does not go through any server of ours.
- **The real camera is on only while it is needed.** The feeder opens your webcam or laptop camera only while some
  program is actually using Tumon Camera, and closes it again afterwards, so the camera's light is off otherwise.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers.**
- **Settings** (chosen camera, resolution, effects, language) are stored locally for your Windows user.
- **Other apps** that receive the picture (TeamTalk, Teams, Zoom and so on) have their own privacy
  policies, which Tumon does not control.

If a future feature needs to send anything off the PC, it will be opt-in, listed here exactly, and
described in both English and Bahasa Indonesia first.
