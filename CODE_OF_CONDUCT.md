# Code of conduct

Tumon follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome people of every background and ability, accept feedback gracefully,
and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

This project exists partly to serve blind and low-vision users, so we especially ask contributors to
treat accessibility reports as high priority, not as feature requests.

To report a conduct problem, use GitHub's private
[security reporting](https://github.com/SkillsHUB-Electronics/tumon/security) form and say it is a
conduct report. The maintainers will handle it confidentially.
